Sterling Thomas Porter

Sterling Thomas Porter, age 36, disappeared on May 25, 1997 from Greensboro, North Carolina, United States. Sterling was last seen entering his Greensboro residence. Investigators suspect foul play, but he has never been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.
