Desmond Moore

Desmond Moore was 24 years old when the disappearance was reported on August 30, 2014. The recorded last-seen location is Gautier, Mississippi, United States. Desmond reportedly left his mother's home to meet friends at a nearby restaurant and did not return. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances. He has not been heard from since.

Investigation

The case is currently recorded as Open / Unresolved — Missing-Person Investigation and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances. The listed investigating agency is Gautier Police Department. Information and tips can be submitted to that agency. The listed contact number is 228-497-8008.
