Jamekia Shaneice Sabb

Jamekia Shaneice Sabb was 16 years old when the disappearance was reported on November 22, 2013. The recorded last-seen location is Charlotte, North Carolina, United States. Jamekia was last seen leaving her residence in the 6200 block of Donna Drive in Charlotte. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. No additional circumstances surrounding her disappearance have been made public.

Investigation

The case is currently recorded as Open / Unresolved — Missing-Person Investigation and is classified as Endangered Missing. The listed investigating agency is Charlotte-Mecklenburg Police Department. Information and tips can be submitted to that agency. The listed contact number is 704-336-3237.
