Jamal Basil Abdul'Faruq

Jamal Basil Abdul'Faruq, age 7, disappeared on April 16, 1990 from Richmond, Virginia, United States. Jamal disappeared while playing with his brother. His brother's body was later found, but he remains missing. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.
