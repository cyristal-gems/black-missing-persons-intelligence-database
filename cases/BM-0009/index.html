BM-0009MissingChild

Adji Desir

Adji Desir, age 6, disappeared on January 10, 2009 from Immokalee, Florida, United States. Adji was left at his grandmother's home in Farm Workers Village while his mother went to work. He was last seen around 5:15 p.m. and has never been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.

Circumstances

Adji was last seen in Immokalee, Florida on January 10, 2009. His mother left him at his grandmother's home in Farm Workers Village in the 800 block of Grace Street that day, as she had to work. Adji was last seen at approximately 5:15 p.m. The current case status is Missing, and the disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
