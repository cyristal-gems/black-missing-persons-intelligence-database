BM-0104MissingChild

Tarasha Benjamin

Tarasha Benjamin was 17 years old when the disappearance was reported on June 26, 2010. The recorded last-seen location is Selma, AL, United States. Tarasha left her Selma home to visit a flea market. Her vehicle was later found abandoned on Route 80 approximately one mile from the market, but she has never been found. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.

Investigation

Selma Police Department is the investigating agency listed in the current public case material. Anyone with information should contact the agency at 334-874-2134.

Timeline

  1. June 26, 2010Tarasha said goodbye to her mother right before she left their Selma, AL home for a flea market on June 2010. That was the last time Tarasha's mother saw her 17-year old daughter.

Physical Description

At the time of the disappearance, Tarasha Benjamin was 17 years old. The record is categorized as Child. Additional physical identifiers should be confirmed through the active source profiles.
