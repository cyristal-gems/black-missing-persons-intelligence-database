Rico Omarr Harris

Rico Omarr Harris was 37 years old when the disappearance was reported on October 10, 2014. The recorded last-seen location is Rumsey, California, United States. Rico last contacted family while driving north through the Sacramento area. His vehicle was later found at a Yolo County regional park near Rumsey, but he remains missing. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances. At the time of the disappearance, Rico Omarr Harris was 37 years old, and the recorded last-seen location was Rumsey, California, United States.

Investigation

The case is currently recorded as Open / Unresolved — Missing-Person Investigation and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances. The listed investigating agency is Yolo County Sheriff's Department. Information and tips can be submitted to that agency. The listed contact number is 530-666-8282.
