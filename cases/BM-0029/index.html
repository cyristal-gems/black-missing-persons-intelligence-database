Kimberly Moore

Kimberly Moore, age 43, disappeared on December 31, 2022 from Ringgold, Louisiana, United States. Kimberly was last seen at a New Year's Eve party in Ringgold while living with Vernon Parker after a recent move from Dallas. She has never been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.

Circumstances

Moore was last seen at a party on Hall Street in Ringgold, Louisiana at 11:30 p.m. on December 31, 2022. She had moved from Dallas, Texas to Louisiana around Thanksgiving of 2022 and was living with a man, Vernon Parker, at the time of her disappearance. Kimberly was last seen at a New Year's Eve party in Ringgold while living with Vernon Parker after a recent move from Dallas. She has never been found.
