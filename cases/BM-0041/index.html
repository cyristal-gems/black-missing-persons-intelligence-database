Andre Terrence Bryant

Andre Terrence Bryant, age 0, disappeared on March 29, 1989 from Brooklyn, New York, United States. Andre disappeared after his mother was abducted and killed when he was one month old. Authorities have considered whether he was taken for an illegal adoption. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Stranger Abduction.
