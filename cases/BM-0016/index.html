Akia Shawnta Eggleston

Akia Shawnta Eggleston, age 22, disappeared on May 3, 2017 from Baltimore, Maryland, United States. Akia withdrew more than $500 from a Baltimore bank shortly before she vanished, an amount considered unusual for her. She is presumed deceased but has never been found. The case is currently listed as Presumed Deceased and is classified as Suspected Homicide.

Circumstances

Eggleston was last seen in Baltimore, Maryland on May 3, 2017. She went to the bank that day and withdrew over $500 in cash, an unusual amount for her. The bank was three miles from Eggleston's apartment in the 600 block of Cherrycrest Road.
