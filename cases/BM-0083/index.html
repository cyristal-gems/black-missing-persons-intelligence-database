Kierra Westine Barnett

Kierra Westine Barnett was 21 years old when the disappearance was reported on December 20, 2006. The recorded last-seen location is Edward, NC, United States. Kierra's family reported her missing after they had not seen or heard from her for five days. Investigators have indicated that criminal activity may be connected to her disappearance. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.

Investigation

Beaufort County Sheriff's Office is the investigating agency listed in the current public case material. Anyone with information should contact the agency at 252-946-7111.

Timeline

  1. December 20, 2006On December 25th 2006, the Beaufort County Sheriff's Office received a report of a missing person from 6733 Old Sandhill Road in Edward, North Carolina. A family member reported that Kierra Westine Barnett, age 21, had not been seen or heard from since December 20th, 2006.

Physical Description

At the time of the disappearance, Kierra Westine Barnett was 21 years old. The record is categorized as Adult Woman. Additional physical identifiers should be confirmed through the active source profiles.
