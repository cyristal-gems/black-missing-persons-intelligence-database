Steven Curtis Talley

Steven Curtis Talley, age 55, disappeared on July 4, 2024 from Greeley, Colorado, United States. Talley last spoke to his family on July 4, 2024; they believe he was in Greeley, Colorado at the time. His family reported him missing on July 25, after trying to reach him daily for weeks without result. He has never been heard from again. The current case status is Missing, and the disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. Steven last spoke with his family while believed to be in Greeley, after which their daily attempts to reach him went unanswered.

Investigation

The case is currently recorded as Open / Unresolved — Missing-Person Investigation and is classified as Endangered Missing. The listed investigating agency is Weld County Sheriff's Office. Information and tips can be submitted to that agency. The listed contact number is 970-356-4015.
