Ebonee S. Spears

Ebonee S. Spears was 30 years old when the disappearance was reported on January 15, 2016. The recorded last-seen location is Wilmington, North Carolina, United States. Ebonee disappeared without medication, identification, purse, or vehicles and left her daughter behind. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. She has never been found. Spears was 30 years old, and the recorded last-seen location was Wilmington, North Carolina, United States.
