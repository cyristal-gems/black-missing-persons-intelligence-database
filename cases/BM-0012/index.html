Shane Anthony Walker

Shane Anthony Walker, age 1, disappeared on August 10, 1989 from Manhattan, New York, United States. Shane disappeared from the Martin Luther King Jr. Towers playground in Harlem while accompanied by his mother. He has never been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Stranger Abduction.

Circumstances

Shane was last seen at the Martin Luther King Jr. Towers playground located at 113th Street and Lenox Avenue in Harlem in the New York borough of Manhattan on August 10, 1989. He was accompanied by his mother, Rosa Glover. Shane disappeared from the Martin Luther King Jr. Towers playground in Harlem while accompanied by his mother. He has never been found.

Investigation

The case is currently recorded as Open / Unresolved — Abduction Investigation and is classified as Stranger Abduction. The listed investigating agencies are New York Police Department and New York Housing Police Department. Information and tips can be submitted to those agencies. The listed contact numbers are 646-610-6914 and 212-410-8500.
