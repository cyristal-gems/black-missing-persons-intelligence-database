Kaylah Neveah Hunter

Kaylah Neveah Hunter, age 6, disappeared on May 24, 2014 from Detroit, Michigan, United States. Kaylah was last seen in Detroit with her mother and infant half-brother, Kristian. Their mother was later found deceased, but both children remain missing. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.

Circumstances

Kaylah and her infant half-brother Kristian were last seen with their mother in Detroit. Their mother was later found deceased, but the children remain missing. At the time of the disappearance, Kaylah Neveah Hunter was 6 years old, and the recorded last-seen location was Detroit, Michigan, United States. The case is classified as Endangered Missing.
