BM-0103MissingChild

Ja'Niyah Aubrielle McMichael

Ja'Niyah Aubrielle McMichael was 13 years old when the disappearance was reported on August 11, 2024. The recorded last-seen location is Gary, Indiana, United States. Ja'Niyah was last seen at her home in the 1900 block of Malcolm X Boulevard. She may remain in the Gary area or may have traveled to Chicago, and investigators have said she may have been the victim of foul play. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.

Investigation

The case is currently recorded as Open / Unresolved — Missing-Person Investigation and is classified as Endangered Missing. The listed investigating agency is Gary Police Department. Information and tips can be submitted to that agency. The listed contact number is 219-881-7300.

Timeline

  1. August 11, 2024Ja'Niyah was last seen at her home in the 1900 block of Malcolm X Boulevard in Gary. She was wearing black pajama pants, a black hooded sweatshirt, and red and black shoes.
  2. Foul Play SuspectedInvestigators have said she may have been the victim of foul play, and the Gary Police Department and FBI continue to investigate her disappearance.

Physical Description

At the time of the disappearance, Ja'Niyah Aubrielle McMichael was 13 years old. The record is categorized as Child. Additional physical identifiers should be confirmed through the active source profiles.
