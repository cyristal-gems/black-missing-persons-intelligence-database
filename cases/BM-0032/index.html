Robert Lee Archie

Robert Lee Archie, age 29, disappeared on December 19, 2001 from Jemison, Alabama, United States. Robert left his Jemison residence for a walk along Chilton County Road 785 and never returned. He has never been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.

Circumstances

Archie was last seen in Jemison, Alabama on December 19, 2001. He left his residence and went for a walk on Chilton County Road 785, and never returned. Robert left his Jemison residence for a walk along Chilton County Road 785 and never returned. He has never been found.

Investigation
