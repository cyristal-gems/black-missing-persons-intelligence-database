BM-0101MissingChild

Makiah Bailey

Makiah Bailey was 16 years old when the disappearance was reported on June 14, 2023. The recorded last-seen location is Baltimore, MD, United States. Makiah disappeared from northwest Baltimore and has not been seen or heard from since. Her family believes she may have remained in the area in the company of an adult man. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.

Investigation

Baltimore City Police Department is the investigating agency listed in the current public case material. Anyone with information should contact the agency at (410) 396-3151.

Timeline

  1. June 14, 2023Makiah Bailey went missing from the northwest area of Baltimore on June 14, 2023, and has not been seen or heard from since. She is an honor roll student approaching her senior year in high school.

Physical Description

At the time of the disappearance, Makiah Bailey was 16 years old. The record is categorized as Child. Additional physical identifiers should be confirmed through the active source profiles.
