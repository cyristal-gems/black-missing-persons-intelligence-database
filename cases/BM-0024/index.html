Dannette Latonia Millbrook

Dannette Latonia Millbrook, age 15, disappeared on March 18, 1990 from Augusta, Georgia, United States. Dannette was last seen with her twin sister at a Pump-N-Shop gas station in Augusta. Neither sister has ever been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.

Circumstances

Dannette and her fraternal twin sister, Jeannette, were last seen at a Pump-N-Shop gas station on the corner of 12th Street and Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard in Augusta, Georgia on March 18, 1990. Dannette was last seen with her twin sister at a Pump-N-Shop gas station in Augusta. Neither sister has ever been found.
