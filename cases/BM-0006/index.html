BM-0006MissingChild

Teekah Latres Lewis

Teekah Latres Lewis, age 2, disappeared on January 23, 1999 from Tacoma, Washington, United States. Teekah was spending the evening at a Tacoma bowling alley with nearly a dozen relatives when she disappeared. She has never been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Stranger Abduction.

Circumstances

Teekah and nearly a dozen of her family spent the evening of January 23, 1999 at New Frontier Lanes bowling alley on Center Street in Tacoma, Washington. Teekah was spending the evening at a Tacoma bowling alley with nearly a dozen relatives when she disappeared. She has never been found.

Investigation

The case is currently recorded as Open / Unresolved — Abduction Investigation and is classified as Stranger Abduction. The listed investigating agencies are Tacoma Police Department and Federal Bureau of Investigation. Information and tips can be submitted to those agencies. The listed contact numbers are 253-798-4721 and 202-324-3000.
