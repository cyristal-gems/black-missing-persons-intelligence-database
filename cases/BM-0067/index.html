Sherri Lee Truesdale

Sherri Lee Truesdale, age 14, disappeared on June 13, 1970 from Winston-Salem, North Carolina, United States. Sherri took a bus into downtown Winston-Salem and never returned home. A lengthy search found no trace of her. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.
