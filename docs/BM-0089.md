# Montell Aker

**Case ID:** BM-0089

## Case Summary

Montell Aker was 19 years old when the disappearance was reported on August 18, 2011. The recorded last-seen location is Urbana, IL, United States. Montell left his Urbana home saying he planned to visit friends, possibly in Rantoul, and never returned. He had no cellphone or known access to money and stopped posting online. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
