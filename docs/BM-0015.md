# Unique RaQuel-Leona Harris

**Case ID:** BM-0015

## Overview

Unique RaQuel-Leona Harris, age 24, disappeared on October 9, 2010 from Washington D.C., Washington D.C., United States. Unique was last seen at the Washington, D.C., apartment she shared with her two young sons only five weeks after moving in. She is presumed deceased but has never been found. The case is currently listed as Presumed Deceased and is classified as Suspected Homicide.
