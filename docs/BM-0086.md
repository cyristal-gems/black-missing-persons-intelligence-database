# Leon Allan Addison

**Case ID:** BM-0086

## Case Summary

Leon Allan Addison was 29 years old when the disappearance was reported on September 5, 2012. The recorded last-seen location is Durham, NC, United States. Leon left his Durham residence for a walk without his cellphone or wallet and never returned. He has never been found. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
