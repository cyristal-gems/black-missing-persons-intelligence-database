# Arianna Fitts

**Case ID:** BM-0005

## Overview

Arianna Fitts, age 2, disappeared on February 1, 2016 from Oakland, California, United States. Arianna disappeared as a toddler two months before her mother, Nicole Fitts, vanished after leaving work in San Francisco. Neither mother nor daughter has been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.

## Circumstances

Arianna's mother, 32-year-old Nicole Fitts, disappeared from San Francisco, California on April 1, 2016. She was last seen after her shift at Best Buy on Harrison Street that day. Arianna disappeared as a toddler two months before her mother, Nicole Fitts, vanished after leaving work in San Francisco. Neither mother nor daughter has been found.
