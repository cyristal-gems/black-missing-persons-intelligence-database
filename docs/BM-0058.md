# Garfield James Meekins

**Case ID:** BM-0058

## Case Summary

Garfield James Meekins was 80 years old when the disappearance was reported on September 6, 1999. The recorded last-seen location is Baltimore, Maryland, United States. Garfield left his Baltimore residence to walk to a local store and did not return. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. He has never been found. At the time of the disappearance, Garfield James Meekins was 80 years old, and the recorded last-seen location was Baltimore, Maryland, United States.
