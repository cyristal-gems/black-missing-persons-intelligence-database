# Jahi Marques Turner

**Case ID:** BM-0040

## Overview

Jahi Marques Turner, age 2, disappeared on April 25, 2002 from San Diego, California, United States. Jahi was reported missing from Balboa Park, although investigators documented conflicting accounts of what happened. Foul play is suspected, and his remains have never been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Suspected Homicide.
