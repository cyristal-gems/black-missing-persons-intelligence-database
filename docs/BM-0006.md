# Teekah Latres Lewis

**Case ID:** BM-0006

## Case Summary

Teekah Latres Lewis was 2 years old when the disappearance was reported on January 23, 1999. The recorded last-seen location is Tacoma, Washington, United States. Teekah and nearly a dozen of her family spent the evening of January 23, 1999 at New Frontier Lanes bowling alley on Center Street in Tacoma, Washington. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Stranger Abduction. Teekah was spending the evening at a Tacoma bowling alley with nearly a dozen relatives when she disappeared. She has never been found.
