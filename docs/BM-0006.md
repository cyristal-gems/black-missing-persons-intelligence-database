# Teekah Latres Lewis

**Case ID:** BM-0006

## Overview

Teekah Latres Lewis, age 2, disappeared on January 23, 1999 from Tacoma, Washington, United States. Teekah was spending the evening at a Tacoma bowling alley with nearly a dozen relatives when she disappeared. She has never been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Stranger Abduction.
