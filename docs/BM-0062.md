Devin "Sacoya" Cooper, age 33, disappeared on September 1, 2021 from Columbus, Ohio, United States. Devin left home to visit a corner store and never returned. Her vehicle was recovered, and the FBI has offered a reward for information about her disappearance. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.
