# Dashad "Sage" Smith

## Overview

Dashad "Sage" Smith, age 19, disappeared on November 20, 2012 from Charlottesville, Virginia, United States. Dashad vanished after planning to meet an acquaintance. Police later treated her disappearance as a homicide investigation, but her body has never been found. The case is currently listed as Presumed Deceased and is classified as Suspected Homicide.
