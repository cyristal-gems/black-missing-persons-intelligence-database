# Alexis D. Ware

**Case ID:** BM-0061

## Overview

Alexis D. Ware, age 29, disappeared on January 30, 2022 from Anderson, South Carolina, United States. Alexis was last seen near an Anderson County 7-Eleven after meeting the father of one of her children. Her vehicle was later captured on highway cameras, but she remains missing. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.
