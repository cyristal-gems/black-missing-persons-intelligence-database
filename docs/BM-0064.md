# Stephanie Marie Gant-Brady

**Case ID:** BM-0064

## Case Summary

Stephanie Marie Gant-Brady was 39 years old when the disappearance was reported on April 8, 1984. The recorded last-seen location is Baltimore, Maryland, United States. Stephanie, a Maryland National Guard trailblazer who had multiple sclerosis, disappeared without a passport and left mobility aids and belongings behind. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. Stephanie disappeared without her passport, leaving behind mobility aids she used because of multiple sclerosis and other personal belongings. She has never been found.
