# Stephanie Marie Gant-Brady

**Case ID:** BM-0064

## Overview

Stephanie Marie Gant-Brady, age 39, disappeared on April 8, 1984 from Baltimore, Maryland, United States. Stephanie disappeared without her passport, leaving behind mobility aids she used because of multiple sclerosis and other personal belongings. She has never been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.
