# Ilonda Banks

## Case Summary

Ilonda Banks was 20 years old when the disappearance was reported on December 28, 2013. The recorded last-seen location is Lithonia, GA, United States. Ilonda was last seen around 1:00 p.m. at a home on Kimberland Gardens Lane in Lithonia. Public case material indicates that she has a cognitive disability. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
