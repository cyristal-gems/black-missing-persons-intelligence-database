# Shaniece Mona Briggs

**Case ID:** BM-0057

## Overview

Shaniece Mona Briggs, age 26, disappeared on June 19, 2013 from Columbus, Ohio, United States. Shaniece left four young children and her personal belongings behind. A witness reported seeing her being pushed into a black vehicle. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Suspected Abduction.
