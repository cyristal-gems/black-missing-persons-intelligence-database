# Shameeka M. Gordon-Dixon

**Case ID:** BM-0027

## Case Summary

Shameeka M. Gordon-Dixon was 20 years old when the disappearance was reported on April 21, 2004. The recorded last-seen location is Staten Island, New York, United States. Gordon-Dixon was last seen at approximately 1:30 a.m. on April 21, 2004 at a relative's home in the vicinity of York Avenue and Richmond Terrace in the New York City borough of Staten Island. She left to visit a friend and never returned. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances. She has not been heard from since.
