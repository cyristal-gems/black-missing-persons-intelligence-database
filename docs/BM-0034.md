# Steven Curtis Talley

**Case ID:** BM-0034

## Overview

Steven Curtis Talley, age 55, disappeared on July 4, 2024 from Greeley, Colorado, United States. Steven last spoke with his family while believed to be in Greeley, after which their daily attempts to reach him went unanswered. He has never been heard from again. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.
