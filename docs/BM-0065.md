# Rico Omarr Harris

**Case ID:** BM-0065

## Overview

Rico Omarr Harris, age 37, disappeared on October 10, 2014 from Rumsey, California, United States. Rico last contacted family while driving north through the Sacramento area. His vehicle was later found at a regional park near Rumsey, but he remains missing. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.
