# Dannette Latonia Millbrook

**Case ID:** BM-0024

## Overview

Dannette Latonia Millbrook, age 15, disappeared on March 18, 1990 from Augusta, Georgia, United States. Dannette was last seen with her twin sister at a Pump-N-Shop gas station in Augusta. Neither sister has ever been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.
