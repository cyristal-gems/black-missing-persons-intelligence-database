# Stacey Lynch

**Case ID:** BM-0031

## Case Summary

Stacey Lynch was 19 years old when the disappearance was reported on December 22, 2020. The recorded last-seen location is Talladega, Alabama, United States. Lynch was last seen leaving his residence at Knoxville Homes Apartments in Talladega, Alabama on December 22, 2020. He has never been heard from again. After his disappearance, a vehicle believed to be connected to his case was found abandoned and burned. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.
