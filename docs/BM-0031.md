# Stacey Lynch

**Case ID:** BM-0031

## Overview

Stacey Lynch, age 19, disappeared on December 22, 2020 from Talladega, Alabama, United States. Stacey left his Talladega apartment and was never heard from again. A vehicle believed to be connected to his disappearance was later found abandoned and burned. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.

## Circumstances

Lynch was last seen leaving his residence at Knoxville Homes Apartments in Talladega, Alabama on December 22, 2020. He has never been heard from again. After his disappearance, a vehicle believed to be connected to his case was found abandoned and burned. Stacey left his Talladega apartment and was never heard from again.
