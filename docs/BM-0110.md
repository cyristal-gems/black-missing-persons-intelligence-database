# Tirrel (Mia) Santiago

**Case ID:** BM-0110

## Case Summary

Tirrel (Mia) Santiago was 16 years old when the disappearance was reported on August 20, 2005. The recorded last-seen location is Brooklyn, NY, United States. Mia was last seen entering a cab near West 10th Street and Hudson Avenue in Brooklyn with an unknown man. Her family reported her missing five days later, and she remains missing. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
