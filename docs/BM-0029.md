# Kimberly Moore

**Case ID:** BM-0029

## Overview

Kimberly Moore, age 43, disappeared on December 31, 2022 from Ringgold, Louisiana, United States. Kimberly was last seen at a New Year's Eve party in Ringgold while living with Vernon Parker after a recent move from Dallas. She has never been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.
