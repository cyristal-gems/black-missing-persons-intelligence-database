# Makiah Bailey

**Case ID:** BM-0101

## Case Summary

Makiah Bailey was 16 years old when the disappearance was reported on June 14, 2023. The recorded last-seen location is Baltimore, MD, United States. Makiah disappeared from northwest Baltimore and has not been seen or heard from since. Her family believes she may have remained in the area in the company of an adult man. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
