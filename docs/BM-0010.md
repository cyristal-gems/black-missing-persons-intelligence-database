# Jaliek L. Rainwalker

**Case ID:** BM-0010

## Overview

Jaliek L. Rainwalker, age 12, disappeared on November 1, 2007 from Greenwich, New York, United States. Jaliek was last seen at his adoptive grandparents' home, where he spent the night alone with his adoptive father. He has never been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.

## Circumstances

Jaliek L. Rainwalker, age 12, disappeared on November 1, 2007 from Greenwich, New York, United States. Jaliek was last seen at a home belonging to his adoptive father's parents in the 10 block of Hill Street in Greenwich, New York on November 1, 2007. His adoptive father, Stephen Burrell Kerr, spent the night alone in the house with him. Jaliek was last seen at his adoptive grandparents' home, where he spent the night alone with his adoptive father. He has never been found.
