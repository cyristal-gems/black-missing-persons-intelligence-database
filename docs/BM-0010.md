# Jaliek L. Rainwalker

**Case ID:** BM-0010

## Case Summary

Jaliek L. Rainwalker was 12 years old when the disappearance was reported on November 1, 2007. The recorded last-seen location is Greenwich, New York, United States. Jaliek was last seen at a home belonging to his adoptive father's parents in the 10 block of Hill Street in Greenwich, New York on November 1, 2007. His adoptive father, Stephen Burrell Kerr, spent the night alone in the house with him. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances. He has never been found.
