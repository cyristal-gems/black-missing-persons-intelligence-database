# Joanna Wright

**Case ID:** BM-0056

## Case Summary

Joanna Wright was 33 years old when the disappearance was reported on December 18, 2016. The recorded last-seen location is Chicago, Illinois, United States. The profile reports Joanna was taken against her will by several unknown men and possibly placed in a blue Mazda. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Stranger Abduction. She has never been found.
