# Joanna Wright

**Case ID:** BM-0056

## Overview

Joanna Wright, age 33, disappeared on December 18, 2016 from Chicago, Illinois, United States. Joanna was reportedly taken against her will by several unknown men and may have been placed in a blue Mazda. She has never been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Stranger Abduction.
