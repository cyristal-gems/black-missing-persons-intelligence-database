# Myra Rena Lewis

**Case ID:** BM-0007

## Overview

Myra Rena Lewis, age 2, disappeared on March 1, 2014 from Camden, Mississippi, United States. Myra was last seen playing outside her home before her mother left for the grocery store and told the children to go inside. She vanished during that period and remains missing. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.

## Circumstances

Myra was last seen playing outside her home on Mount Pilgrim Road in Camden, Mississippi between 10:30 and 11:00 a.m. on March 1, 2014. Her mother left to go to the grocery store and told Myra and her sisters to go inside the house, where their father was. Myra was last seen playing outside her home before her mother left for the grocery store and told the children to go inside. She vanished during that period and remains missing.
