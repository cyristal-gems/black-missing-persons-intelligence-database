# Tabitha Yvette Queen

**Case ID:** BM-0054

## Case Summary

Tabitha Yvette Queen, age 29, disappeared on May 10, 2021 from Bastrop, Louisiana, United States. She was last seen on surveillance video at a Save-U-More grocery store in Bastrop. Her vehicle was later found abandoned on West Pine Street after an unidentified Black man parked it and walked away. The current case status is Missing, and the disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. She was reported missing the following day. Police identified a person of interest but have announced no arrest.
