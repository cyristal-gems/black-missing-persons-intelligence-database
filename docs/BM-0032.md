# Robert Lee Archie

**Case ID:** BM-0032

## Case Summary

Robert Lee Archie was 29 years old when the disappearance was reported on December 19, 2001. The recorded last-seen location is Jemison, Alabama, United States. Archie was last seen in Jemison, Alabama on December 19, 2001. He left his residence and went for a walk on Chilton County Road 785, and never returned. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. He has never been found.
