# D'Wan Christian Sims

**Case ID:** BM-0038

## Overview

D'Wan Christian Sims, age 4, disappeared on December 11, 1994 from Livonia, Michigan, United States. D'Wan was reported missing from a Livonia shopping mall, but investigators said they did not believe he had been there that day. His disappearance remains unresolved. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.
