# D'Wan Christian Sims

**Case ID:** BM-0038

## Case Summary

D'Wan Christian Sims was 4 years old when the disappearance was reported on December 11, 1994. The recorded last-seen location is Livonia, Michigan, United States. D'Wan was reported missing from a Livonia shopping mall. Investigators stated they did not believe he had been at the mall that day; the case remains open. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances. His disappearance remains unresolved.
