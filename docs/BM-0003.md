# Tionda Z. Bradley

**Case ID:** BM-0003

## Case Summary

Tionda Z. Bradley was 10 years old when the disappearance was reported on July 6, 2001. The recorded last-seen location is Chicago, Illinois, United States. Tionda was last seen at her family's residence in the 3500 block of South Lake Park Avenue in Chicago, Illinois on July 6, 2001. Her mother departed for work at approximately 6:30 a.m. The current case status is Missing, and the disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. She has not been seen since. The current investigative status is Open / Unresolved — Missing-Person Investigation.
