# Tionda Z. Bradley

**Case ID:** BM-0003

## Overview

Tionda Z. Bradley, age 10, disappeared on July 6, 2001 from Chicago, Illinois, United States. Tionda was last seen at her family's South Lake Park Avenue residence after her mother left for work around 6:30 a.m. She has not been seen since. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.
