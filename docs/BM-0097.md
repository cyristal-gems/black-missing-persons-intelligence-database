# Karl Davon Ballinger

**Case ID:** BM-0097

## Case Summary

Karl Davon Ballinger was 41 years old when the disappearance was reported on April 15, 2023. The recorded last-seen location is Barstow, CA, United States. Karl was last seen leaving work in Rialto. His vehicle was later found abandoned on a highway near Ludlow, approximately 50 miles from Barstow. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
