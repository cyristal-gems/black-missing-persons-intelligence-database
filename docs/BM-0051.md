# Desmond Moore

**Case ID:** BM-0051

## Overview

Desmond Moore, age 24, disappeared on August 30, 2014 from Gautier, Mississippi, United States. Desmond left his mother's Gautier home to meet friends at a nearby restaurant and never returned. He has not been heard from since. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.
