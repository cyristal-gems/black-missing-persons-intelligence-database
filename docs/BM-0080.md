# Jennifer Joyce Barton

**Case ID:** BM-0080

## Case Summary

Jennifer Joyce Barton was 20 years old when the disappearance was reported on May 16, 1976. The recorded last-seen location is Austin, TX, United States. Jennifer had gone to the movies with a friend and was later seen near 11th and Waller Streets speaking with a man. She has never been found. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
