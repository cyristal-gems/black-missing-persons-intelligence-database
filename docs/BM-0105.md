# De'Nita Barrett

**Case ID:** BM-0105

## Case Summary

De'Nita Barrett was 13 years old when the disappearance was reported on November 27, 2012. The recorded last-seen location is Baltimore, MD, United States. De'Nita was last seen around 7:15 a.m. in the 900 block of North Linwood Avenue in Baltimore. She has not been seen since. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
