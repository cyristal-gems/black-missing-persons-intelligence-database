# Shawn Corneilus Alston

**Case ID:** BM-0093

## Case Summary

Shawn Corneilus Alston was 39 years old when the disappearance was reported on October 18, 2012. The recorded last-seen location is Garysburg, NC, United States. Shawn left a friend's Garysburg home to walk to his nearby residence and was not seen again. Searches of the surrounding wooded area and an aerial search found no trace of him. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
