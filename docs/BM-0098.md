# Yasmin Rayon Acree

**Case ID:** BM-0098

## Case Summary

Yasmin Rayon Acree was 15 years old when the disappearance was reported on January 15, 2008. The recorded last-seen location is Chicago, IL, United States. Yasmin was last seen at her Chicago home with her mother's boyfriend. She has never been found. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
