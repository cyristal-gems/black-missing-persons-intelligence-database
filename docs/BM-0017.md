# Phoenix Lucille Coldon

**Case ID:** BM-0017

## Case Summary

Phoenix Lucille Coldon was 23 years old when the disappearance was reported on December 18, 2011. The recorded last-seen location is Spanish Lake, Missouri, United States. Coldon was last seen sitting in her car in her driveway in the 12600 block of Countrybrook Drive in Spanish Lake, Missouri on December 18, 2011. Her father saw her drive away at 2:20 p.m. The current case status is Missing, and the disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. She has never been seen again. The current investigative status is Open / Unresolved — Missing-Person Investigation.
