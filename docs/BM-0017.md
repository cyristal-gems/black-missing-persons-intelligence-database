# Phoenix Lucille Coldon

**Case ID:** BM-0017

## Overview

Phoenix Lucille Coldon, age 23, disappeared on December 18, 2011 from Spanish Lake, Missouri, United States. Phoenix was sitting in her car in the driveway before her father watched her drive away around 2:20 p.m. She has never been seen again. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.
