# Jaquilla Evonne Scales

**Case ID:** BM-0018

## Overview

Jaquilla Evonne Scales, age 4, disappeared on September 5, 2001 from Wichita, Kansas, United States. Jaquilla was seen asleep in her bed around 12:30 a.m. and discovered missing approximately two and a half hours later. She has never been seen again. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.
