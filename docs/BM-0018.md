# Jaquilla Evonne Scales

**Case ID:** BM-0018

## Case Summary

Jaquilla Evonne Scales was 4 years old when the disappearance was reported on September 5, 2001. The recorded last-seen location is Wichita, Kansas, United States. Jaquilla was last seen asleep in her bed at home at about 12:30 a.m. and was discovered missing at about 3:00 a.m. She has not been seen since. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. She has never been seen again.
