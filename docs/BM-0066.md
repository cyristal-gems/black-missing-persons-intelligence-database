# Anthony Tyrone Lee

**Case ID:** BM-0066

## Case Summary

Anthony Tyrone Lee was 33 years old when the disappearance was reported on June 25, 2011. The recorded last-seen location is Longview, Texas, United States. Anthony disappeared from his Longview residence. The home appeared to have been left suddenly, with personal belongings and money left behind. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.
