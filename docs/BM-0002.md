# Relisha Tenau Rudd

**Case ID:** BM-0002

## Overview

Relisha Tenau Rudd, age 8, disappeared on March 1, 2014 from Washington D.C., Washington D.C., United States. Relisha was last seen in Washington, D.C., while in the care of Kahlil Malik Tatum after her family had lived in a homeless shelter for more than a year. She has never been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.
