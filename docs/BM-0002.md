# Relisha Tenau Rudd

**Case ID:** BM-0002

## Case Summary

Relisha Tenau Rudd was 8 years old when the disappearance was reported on March 1, 2014. The recorded last-seen location is Washington D.C., United States. Relisha was last seen in Washington D.C. in March 2014. She was in the care of Kahlil Malik Tatum at the time. For over a year Relisha's family had been residing in a homeless shelter in the former D.C. The current case status is Missing, and the disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. She has never been found.
