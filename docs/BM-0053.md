# Kimberly Nicole Arrington

**Case ID:** BM-0053

## Case Summary

Kimberly Nicole Arrington, age 16, disappeared on October 30, 1998 from Montgomery, Alabama, United States. Kimberly was last seen after leaving to walk to a nearby store in Montgomery. Her current NCMEC poster continues to list her as missing. The current case status is Missing, and the disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. Kimberly left to walk to a nearby Montgomery store and never returned. She remains missing.
