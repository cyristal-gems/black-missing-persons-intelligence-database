# Kimberly Nicole Arrington

**Case ID:** BM-0053

## Overview

Kimberly Nicole Arrington, age 16, disappeared on October 30, 1998 from Montgomery, Alabama, United States. Kimberly left to walk to a nearby Montgomery store and never returned. She remains missing. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.
