# Shane Anthony Walker

**Case ID:** BM-0012

## Case Summary

Shane Anthony Walker, age 1, disappeared on August 10, 1989 from Manhattan, New York, United States. Shane was last seen at the Martin Luther King Jr. Towers playground located at 113th Street and Lenox Avenue in Harlem in the New York borough of Manhattan on August 10, 1989. He was accompanied by his mother, Rosa Glover. The current case status is Missing, and the disappearance is classified as Stranger Abduction. Towers playground in Harlem while accompanied by his mother. He has never been found.
