# Christopher Milton Dansby

**Case ID:** BM-0011

## Overview

Christopher Milton Dansby, age 2, disappeared on May 18, 1989 from Manhattan, New York, United States. Christopher disappeared from the Martin Luther King Jr. Towers playground in Harlem. He has never been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Stranger Abduction.
