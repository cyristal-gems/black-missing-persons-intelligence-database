# Edna Mae Bland

**Case ID:** BM-0085

## Case Summary

Edna Mae Bland was 33 years old when the disappearance was reported on July 6, 1999. The recorded last-seen location is Baltimore, MD, United States. Edna was last seen at her home on Hollins Street in Baltimore. No additional circumstances surrounding her disappearance have been made public. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
