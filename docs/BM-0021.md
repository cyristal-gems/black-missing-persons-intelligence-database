# Kierra Michelle Coles

**Case ID:** BM-0021

## Overview

Kierra Michelle Coles, age 26, disappeared on October 2, 2018 from Chicago, Illinois, United States. Kierra never arrived at the post office where she worked after calling in sick that day. She has not been heard from again. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.

## Circumstances

Coles was last seen in the Chatham neighborhood of Chicago, Illinois on October 2, 2018. She never arrived at the post office where she worked as a city carrier assistant, and she had actually called in sick that day. She has never been heard from again.
