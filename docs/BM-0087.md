# Dion Andre Afeworki

**Case ID:** BM-0087

## Case Summary

Dion Andre Afeworki was 30 years old when the disappearance was reported on June 22, 2015. The recorded last-seen location is Pasadena, CA, United States. Dion was last seen at the Allen Avenue Gold Line station in Pasadena around 11:00 a.m. He has schizophrenia and depression and requires medication. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. The current investigative status is Open / Unresolved — Missing-Person Investigation.
