# LaQuanta Nachelle Riley

**Case ID:** BM-0050

## Overview

LaQuanta Nachelle Riley, age 19, disappeared on December 7, 2003 from Montgomery, Alabama, United States. LaQuanta was last seen leaving her Montgomery residence in a dark green four-door vehicle with unidentified people. She has not been seen since. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.
