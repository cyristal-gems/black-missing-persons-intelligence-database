# LaQuanta Nachelle Riley

**Case ID:** BM-0050

## Case Summary

LaQuanta Nachelle Riley was 19 years old when the disappearance was reported on December 7, 2003. The recorded last-seen location is Montgomery, Alabama, United States. LaQuanta was last seen leaving her residence in Montgomery in a dark green four-door vehicle with unidentified people. She has not been seen since. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.
