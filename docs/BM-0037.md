# Tyarra Cacique Williams

**Case ID:** BM-0037

## Overview

Tyarra Cacique Williams, age 19, disappeared on January 7, 2016 from Greensboro, North Carolina, United States. Tyarra left the Stoneybrook Apartments saying she planned to visit a friend. Her phone's GPS signal stopped, and she never returned home. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.
