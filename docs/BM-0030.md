# Logan Drew Schiendelman

**Case ID:** BM-0030

## Overview

Logan Drew Schiendelman, age 19, disappeared on May 19, 2016 from Tumwater, Washington, United States. Logan was last seen in Tumwater and has never been heard from again. No confirmed trace of him has been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.
