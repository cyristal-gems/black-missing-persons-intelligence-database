# Terence Akins

## Case Summary

Terence Akins was 41 years old when the disappearance was reported on September 29, 2020. The recorded last-seen location is Long Beach, CA, United States. Terence was last seen at his residence in the 1900 block of Atlantic Avenue in Long Beach. No additional circumstances surrounding his disappearance have been made public. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
