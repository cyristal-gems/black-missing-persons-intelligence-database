# Keeshae Eunique Jacobs

**Case ID:** BM-0042

## Overview

Keeshae Eunique Jacobs, age 21, disappeared on September 26, 2016 from Richmond, Virginia, United States. Keeshae vanished after saying she planned to stay with a friend. Police later found her blood and phone SIM card inside a residence, and foul play is suspected. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.
