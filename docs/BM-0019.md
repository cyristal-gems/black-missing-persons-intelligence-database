# Laresha Deana Walker

**Case ID:** BM-0019

## Case Summary

Laresha Deana Walker, age 23, disappeared on November 19, 1999 from Nashville, Tennessee, United States. Laresha was last seen in her hometown of Nashville, Tennessee on November 19, 1999. She dropped her two-year-old son off at her sister's residence in the 900 block of Gayle Street for the night. The current case status is Missing, and the disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. Laresha dropped her two-year-old son off at her sister's Nashville home for the night. She disappeared afterward and has never been found.
