# Laresha Deana Walker

**Case ID:** BM-0019

## Overview

Laresha Deana Walker, age 23, disappeared on November 19, 1999 from Nashville, Tennessee, United States. Laresha dropped her two-year-old son off at her sister's Nashville home for the night. She disappeared afterward and has never been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.
