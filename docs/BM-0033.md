# Typhenie Kae Johnson

**Case ID:** BM-0033

## Case Summary

Typhenie Kae Johnson was 25 years old when she disappeared from the Post Oak East Apartments in Euless, Texas, on October 10, 2016. Witnesses last saw her at approximately 9 p.m. speaking with her former boyfriend, Christopher Revill, in the apartment complex parking lot. Family members and friends who were inside an apartment said the discussion appeared to become an argument. Revill was later seen alone near the open trunk of his white Ford Taurus, and he drove away after closing the trunk. Johnson's cell phone and a sock were found on the ground near the parking area, while her keys were found on top of her vehicle. Authorities suspected foul play, and Revill was detained after returning to the apartment complex later that night. In August 2019, Revill was convicted of aggravated kidnapping and sentenced to life in prison, but Johnson remains missing.

## Investigation

Fort Worth Police lists Johnson's disappearance as an open long-term missing-person case under report number 160097006. Investigators suspected foul play after her belongings were found near the location where witnesses last saw her with Christopher Revill. Revill was detained after returning to the apartment complex and was later prosecuted in connection with the disappearance. On August 21, 2019, he was convicted of aggravated kidnapping and sentenced to life in prison. The conviction did not establish Johnson's location, and Texas DPS continues to list her as involuntarily missing.
