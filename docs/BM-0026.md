# LaKisha Shantella Taylor

**Case ID:** BM-0026

## Case Summary

LaKisha Shantella Taylor, age 27, disappeared on June 13, 2008 from Lake Charles, Louisiana, United States. Taylor was last seen in Lake Charles, Louisiana on June 13, 2008. She lived with her cousin at the time of her disappearance. Her cousin went out for breakfast and when she returned, Taylor was gone. The current case status is Missing, and the disappearance is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances. LaKisha was gone when her cousin returned to their Lake Charles home after going out for breakfast. She has never been found.
