# LaKisha Shantella Taylor

**Case ID:** BM-0026

## Overview

LaKisha Shantella Taylor, age 27, disappeared on June 13, 2008 from Lake Charles, Louisiana, United States. LaKisha was gone when her cousin returned to their Lake Charles home after going out for breakfast. She has never been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.
