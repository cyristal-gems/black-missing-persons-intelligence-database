# Jessie Barnes

**Case ID:** BM-0082

## Case Summary

Jessie Barnes was 28 years old when the disappearance was reported on July 7, 2000. The recorded last-seen location is West Point, MS, United States. Jessie was last seen late in the afternoon at a family member's residence on Little Street in West Point. She has a medical condition and has never been found. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
