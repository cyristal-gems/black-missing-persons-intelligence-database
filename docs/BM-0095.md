# Brian Kelvin Andrewin

**Case ID:** BM-0095

## Case Summary

Brian Kelvin Andrewin was 16 years old when the disappearance was reported on July 10, 1995. The recorded last-seen location is Chicago, IL, United States. Brian was last seen playing basketball with friends at a Chicago park. He has not been seen or heard from since. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
