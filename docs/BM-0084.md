# Latrice Lacole Belton

## Case Summary

Latrice Lacole Belton was 32 years old when the disappearance was reported on April 11, 2013. The recorded last-seen location is Sacramento, CA, United States. Latrice was last seen in the 4300 block of Stockton Boulevard in Sacramento. No additional circumstances surrounding her disappearance have been made public. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Involuntary Missing.
