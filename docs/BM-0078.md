# Lekisha Allen

**Case ID:** BM-0078

## Case Summary

Lekisha Allen was 37 years old when the disappearance was reported on September 3, 2015. The recorded last-seen location is Detroit, MI, United States. Lekisha was last seen by her brother-in-law near Five Mile Road and Greenfield Road around 1:30 p.m. She has bipolar disorder and schizophrenia and requires medication. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. The current investigative status is Open / Unresolved — Missing-Person Investigation.
