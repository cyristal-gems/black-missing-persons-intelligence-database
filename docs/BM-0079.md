# Constance Anderson

**Case ID:** BM-0079

## Case Summary

Constance Anderson was 19 years old when the disappearance was reported on January 24, 2006. The recorded last-seen location is Mansfield, LA, United States. Constance was last seen at her Mansfield residence around 11:00 p.m. She later called a friend around 1:30 a.m. and said she was coming over, but she never arrived. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. The current investigative status is Open / Unresolved — Missing-Person Investigation.
