# Emmanuel Adebisi

**Case ID:** BM-0107

## Case Summary

Emmanuel Adebisi was 14 years old when the disappearance was reported on June 1, 2015. The recorded last-seen location is Parkville, MD, United States. Emmanuel left his Parkville home and was later seen on the grounds of Loch Raven Academy. Police were called, but he ran away and has not been seen since. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
