# Michael Anthony McClain

**Case ID:** BM-0035

## Overview

Michael Anthony McClain, age 29, disappeared on April 20, 2019 from Nashua, New Hampshire, United States. Michael was last seen at the Tropical Lounge in Nashua while out with friends. He has not been heard from since. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.
