# Adrean Sade Capers

**Case ID:** BM-0028

## Case Summary

Adrean Sade Capers was 28 years old when the disappearance was reported on March 28, 2020. The recorded last-seen location is Summerville, South Carolina, United States. Adrean was last seen at the Econo Lodge in the 100 block of Holiday Drive in Summerville, South Carolina on March 28, 2020. She disappeared after a heated argument with her husband, Harry Capers III, and has never been heard from again. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. Adrean was last seen at a Summerville motel after a heated argument with her husband.
