# Cheniya Baker

**Case ID:** BM-0102

## Case Summary

Cheniya Baker was 14 years old when the disappearance was reported on December 5, 2018. The recorded last-seen location is Philadelphia, PA, United States. Cheniya was last seen by her mother leaving their residence in the 1400 block of South Vodges Street in Philadelphia. She has not been seen since. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
