# Hassani Jamil Campbell

**Case ID:** BM-0014

## Overview

Hassani Jamil Campbell, age 5, disappeared on August 10, 2009 from Oakland, California, United States. Hassani was reportedly last seen in Oakland's Rockridge district around 4:15 p.m. No confirmed trace of him has been found. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Endangered Missing.
