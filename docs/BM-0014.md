# Hassani Jamil Campbell

**Case ID:** BM-0014

## Case Summary

Hassani Jamil Campbell was 5 years old when the disappearance was reported on August 10, 2009. The recorded last-seen location is Oakland, California, United States. Hassani was reportedly last seen in the Rockridge district of Oakland, California at 4:15 p.m. on August 10, 2009. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. No confirmed trace of him has been found.
