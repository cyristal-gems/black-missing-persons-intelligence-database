# Jhessye Jash'ua Shockley

**Case ID:** BM-0039

## Overview

Jhessye Jash'ua Shockley, age 5, disappeared on October 11, 2011 from Glendale, Arizona, United States. Jhessye was reported missing from her Glendale home. Her mother was later convicted of murder and child abuse, but her body has never been found. The case is currently listed as Presumed Deceased and is classified as Suspected Homicide.
