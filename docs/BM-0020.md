# Pamela J. Butler

**Case ID:** BM-0020

## Case Summary

Pamela J. Butler was 47 years old when the disappearance was reported on February 12, 2009. The recorded last-seen location is Washington D.C., United States. Butler was last seen talking to her boyfriend, Jose Angel Rodriguez-Cruz, at her home in the 5800 block of 4th Street northwest in Washington D.C. at 9:48 p.m. on February 12, 2009. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Suspected Homicide. She has never been found.
