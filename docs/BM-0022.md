# Shakeima Ann Cabbagestalk

**Case ID:** BM-0022

## Case Summary

Shakeima Ann Cabbagestalk, age 10, disappeared on July 22, 1993 from Dillon, South Carolina, United States. Shakeima was last seen in Dillon, South Carolina at 5:00 p.m. on July 22, 1993. Her stepfather, Sam Harmon, was the last person to see her. He left with her to go to the Turning Point Grocery on south First Avenue. The current case status is Missing, and the disappearance is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances. Shakeima was last seen leaving with her stepfather to visit a grocery store in Dillon. She has never been found.
