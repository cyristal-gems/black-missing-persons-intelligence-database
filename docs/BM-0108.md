# Taylor Casey

**Case ID:** BM-0108

## Case Summary

Taylor Casey was 41 years old when the disappearance was reported on June 19, 2024. The recorded last-seen location is Nassau, New Providence, Bahamas. Taylor was last seen while attending a yoga retreat in the Paradise Island and Nassau area. Her phone was later found in the water, but she remains missing. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing.
