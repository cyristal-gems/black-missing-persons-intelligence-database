# Raymond Paul Jones

## Case Summary

Raymond Paul Jones was 42 years old when the disappearance was reported on August 4, 2003. The recorded last-seen location is Longview, Texas, United States. Raymond was last seen leaving a relative's residence with two unidentified men in a small white four-door vehicle. He has not been heard from since. The current case status is Missing.
