# Raymond Paul Jones

**Case ID:** BM-0075

## Overview

Raymond Paul Jones, age 42, disappeared on August 4, 2003 from Longview, Texas, United States. Raymond was last seen leaving a relative's Longview residence with two unidentified men in a small white four-door vehicle. He has not been heard from since. The case is currently listed as Missing and is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances.
