# Daniel Robinson

**Case ID:** BM-0074

## Case Summary

Daniel Robinson was 24 years old when the disappearance was reported on June 23, 2021. The recorded last-seen location is Buckeye, Arizona, United States. Daniel, a geologist, left a remote work site and vanished. His damaged vehicle was later found in a ravine; searches have not located him. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Missing Under Mysterious Circumstances. At the time of the disappearance, Daniel Robinson was 24 years old, and the recorded last-seen location was Buckeye, Arizona, United States.
