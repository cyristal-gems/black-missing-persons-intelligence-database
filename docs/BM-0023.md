# Jeannette Latrice Millbrook

**Case ID:** BM-0023

## Case Summary

Jeannette Latrice Millbrook was 15 years old when the disappearance was reported on March 18, 1990. The recorded last-seen location is Augusta, Georgia, United States. Jeannette and her fraternal twin sister, Dannette, were last seen at a Pump-N-Shop gas station on the corner of 12th Street and Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard in Augusta, Georgia on March 18, 1990. The current case status is Missing. The disappearance is classified as Endangered Missing. Neither sister has ever been found.
